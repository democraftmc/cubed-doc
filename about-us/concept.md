# 📦 Our Concept
The first aim of **DEMOCRAFT** is to allow players to play together. We always follow the idea that fames should be play together, without any distinction of hardware, version or accounts differencies. You paid for the game. You have the right to play. 

## 🌂 But we faced an issue
Bedrock and Java edition are really different, with interfaces, gameplay and mechanics which are good one some editions and bad on others. So we choose to add a bit of magic, **a ressource pack** to our server.

The first twist is that we don't have one ressource pack. Actually, we have **2** differents ressource pack, one for each edition which advantages on the other.
