# 🧡 Our Values

Today, it's complicated for french minecraft servers to be economically independent. So, instead of doing the bug business men, we decided to make something fun, both for the players and the staff team

**DEMOCRAFT is open to everyone**. You can connect with what you want, as who you are. We are open to everyone, from the former java player to the non-binary one playing on they connected Samsung fridge. 

**DEMOCRAFT work for you.** Not for the money. Our project is guided by pation, and we hope that our game will inspire other
